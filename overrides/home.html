About

An analyst who explains the why.

I'm a senior business intelligence analyst in utility-scale solar construction, where I build reporting across finance, operations, project controls, engineering, and estimating. I also run an internal training program that teaches non-analysts to work with their own data, so I am used to explaining technical decisions in plain language.

I hold an M.S. in Data Science with a focus on machine learning and AI. Most of my client work today is Power BI: data modelling, DAX, and reports that end in a clear next action. Forecasting and AI work is where I am heading next.

FAQ

Common questions.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Mostly Power BI: new dashboards, fixing or speeding up existing reports, data modelling, and DAX. I also take on SQL and Python analysis work, and I am open to forecasting projects where there is enough history to model.

My data is in spreadsheets. Is that a problem?

No. Several of my projects started from Excel files. Part of the work is turning those into a clean model that refreshes reliably.

How do you price work?

Where the scope is clear I quote a fixed price for a defined deliverable. For open-ended or ongoing work I use a monthly block of hours. Either way you see the number before anything starts.

How do you handle confidential data?

I am happy to sign an NDA before seeing anything, and I work inside your environment where possible. I do not publish client dashboards or data.

What happens after you hand over?

You get documentation covering the data model, the measures, and how to refresh and extend the report. If you want me to stay involved, we can set up ongoing support.
